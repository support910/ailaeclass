## Exam Updates

- Traditional exams and quick practice now require a duration of at least one minute.
- Teachers and administrators can configure the duration when creating or editing an exam.
- Student attempts are submitted automatically when the countdown reaches zero.
- Server-side checks prevent creation or publication of exams without a valid duration.
- The submission endpoint allows a short network grace period after the client countdown expires.
- Every question in the editor is labeled with its current sequence number and renumbers automatically after list changes.

## Course Permission Updates

- A teacher's managed-course list contains only courses where the teacher has tutor or administrator membership.
- Courses outside that management scope remain discoverable through the read-only course preview.
- The preview exposes course description, highlights, structure, and teaching team without exposing management controls or protected course materials.

## Localization

- The timer auto-submit hint and editor question numbering are available in all 15 configured language packs.
